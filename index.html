About Me

I graduated from IIT Kharagpur with an integrated M.Sc in Mathematics & Computing, which is a long way of saying I spent five years learning how to think about problems before writing code for them. These days I'm a Software Engineer at Walmart Global Tech in Bengaluru, where I work on systems that have to be correct at a scale where "mostly correct" is not a thing.

Competitive programming is what got me here. It's still the habit I'm least likely to drop — there's something clarifying about a problem that either passes every test case or doesn't. Outside of that, you'll find me in the gym, chasing a number on a barbell with roughly the same stubbornness I bring to a failing submission.
